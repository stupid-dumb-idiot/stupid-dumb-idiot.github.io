Headers

Headers are like big, bold pieces of text, you can use them to split your website up into sections, for your title, or just if you want large text.

The higher the number you put, the smaller the text gets

    <h1>Hello</h1>
    <h2>Hello</h2>
    <h3>Hello</h3>
    <h4>Hello</h4>
    <h5>Hello</h5>
    <h6>Hello</h6>
